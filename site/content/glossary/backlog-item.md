---
entry: 'Backlog Item'
---

A piece of functionality (often imprecisely called [User Story](/glossary#user-story)). It is the basic unit of work that build up the [Product](/glossary#product-backlog) and [Sprint Backlogs](/glossary#sprint-backlog). A Backlog Item shouldn't be confused with a [Task](/glossary#task) or [Technical Specification](/technical-specification): it should remain firmly in the Functional domain and provide just enough information for the Team to be capable of assessing its feasibility within an [Iteration](/glossary#iteration) and confindently start working on it.
