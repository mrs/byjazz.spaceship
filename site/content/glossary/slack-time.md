---
entry: 'Slack Time'
---

The non-allocated time in an iteration. Used to handle unforeseen work, small improvements, fix [Broken Windows](/glossary#broken-window), \"Pebble in the Shoe\" problems,... At odds with the principle of [Utilisation Maximisation](/glossary#utilisation-maximisation) dear to traditional management methodologies, [Agile](/glossary#agile) approaches recognise the healthy and valuable nature of Slack Time.
