---
entry: 'Development Team'
---

In (Scrum, the team responsible for the implementation of the Product. It includes _all_ the people necessary for the implementation (Designers, Developers, Testers, Analysts,...). In opposition, the [Scrum Team](/glossary#scrum-team) not only includes the people responsible for the implementation, but the [Scrum Master](/glossary#scrum-master) and [Product Owner](/glossary#product-owner) as well.
