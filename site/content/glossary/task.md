---
entry: 'Task'
---

A base unit of work. As opposed to a [Backlog Item](/glossary#backlog-item) or [User Story](/glossary#user-story), a Task is what is picked up and executed by Team members. In [Agile](/glossary#agile) methodologies, Tasks are not assigned to, but taken by team members in a self organizing fashion ([Push vs Pull](/glossary#push-vs-pull)).
