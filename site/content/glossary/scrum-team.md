---
entry: 'Scrum Team'
---

In Scrum, the team responsible for the Product and its implementation. It includes all the people necessary for the implementation (Designers, Developers, Testers, Analysts,...), the [Scrum Master](/glossary#scrum-master), and the [Product Owner](/glossary#product-owner). In opposition, the [Development Team](/glossary#development-team) only includes the people responsible for the implementation.
