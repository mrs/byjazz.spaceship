entry: 'BDD'
---

Behaviour Driven Development. An [Automated Testing](/glossary#automated-testing) focussed approach to software development. It relies on [Functional Specifications](/glossary#functional-specifications) (written as [User Stories](/glossary#user-story) according to the [Gherkin](/glossary#gherkin) syntax) to drive the development effort, similarly to [TDD](/glossary#tdd), but on a broader scale. Since it is concerned with functional pecifications, it can be an opportunity to foster collaboration between the Business and the [Development Team](/glossary#development-team).
