entry: 'Sprint Backlog'
---

In Scrum, amongst the [Artefacts](/glossary#scrum-artefacts), there are two backlogs: the Sprint Backlog and the [Product Backlog](/product-backlog). The Sprint Backlog encapsulates the scope of the current [Sprint](/glossary#sprint) and belongs to the [Development Team](/glossary#development-team).
