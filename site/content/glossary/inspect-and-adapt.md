---
entry: 'Inspect and Adapt'
---

At the core of [Agility](/glossary#agility), inspection and adaptation are two of the most important activities in an empirical process improvement dynamic. Teams will inspect many aspects of their work and process (for instance the [Product Increment](/glossary#product-increment), at the [Sprint Review](/glossary#sprint-review), the Team's process at the [Retrospective](/glossary#retrospective),...) and will adapt as necessary, in fact as close as possible to continuously.
