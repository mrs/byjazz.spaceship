---
entry: 'Scaled Agile'
---

A set of approaches aimed at applying [Agile](/glossary#agile) to large companies and products. Small scale, single team agile practices are well established, but the community is facing struggle in identifying ways to generalise the practices to more complex, more ambitious situations. The main current approaches are [SAFe](/glossary#sa-fe), [Scrum @ Scale](/glossary#scrum-@-scale), and [LeSS](/glossary#le-ss).
