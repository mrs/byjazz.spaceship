I have been fascinated by music for as long as I can remember. I started
learning the guitar as a teenager, and have come back to it over and over
again ever since.

Amongst the many different styles of western music, Jazz has that special
combination of characteristics: it emphasizes improvisation, while
also demanding technical excellence and a deep understanding of music from its players.

I chose the ByJazz name as an analogy between Jazz bands
and high performing agile teams.

Similar to a great Jazz band improvising, a great team will know,
understand and respect each team member's strength and weaknesses. They
will self organize in order to generate the most value relying on that
knowledge. And when push comes to shove, they will improvise together in order to
find a solution to the problem at hand.

In short then, both are examples of multi-disciplinary groups of people technically on
point and capable of improvising when the need arrises.
